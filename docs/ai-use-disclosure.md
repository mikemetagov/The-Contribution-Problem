# AI-use disclosure (draft)
This study was executed by large language model agents under the direction of the principal investigator. Agents performed search, deduplication, screening, data extraction, coding, reliability computation, on-chain data retrieval, synthesis and drafting. Screening and coding used two independent LLM coders in isolated sessions with an adjudication pass; agreement statistics are reported in `reliability/`. The principal investigator approved the protocol, codebook, synthesis logic and final outputs at four gates and audited a 10–15% random sample of coding. Model names and versions are recorded in the coding logs. All interpretations and conclusions are the responsibility of the principal investigator.
